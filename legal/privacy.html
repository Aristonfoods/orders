Privacy Policy - Ariston Stock Check

Last updated: 30 September 2026

"Ariston Stock Check" is a private, internal software tool operated by Ariston Trading Ltd, Unit 6 Mead Park Industrial Estate, Harlow, Essex, CM20 2SE, United Kingdom ("we", "us"). It is not offered to the public or to any other business.

What the tool does

Once a day it reads product and stock-quantity information from our own QuickBooks Online company file and compares it with our own inventory system, so that we can spot differences in our own stock records.

Data we access

Only our own company's product names, SKUs and stock quantities. The tool does not read or store personal data about customers, employees or any other individual, and it does not make any changes in QuickBooks.

Where data is kept

Data is processed on a company computer owned by Ariston Trading Ltd. Results are saved only in our own company files. Access tokens are stored on that computer and are not shared.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties.

Retention and deletion

Results are kept only as long as we need them for our own stock checks. We can disconnect the tool from QuickBooks at any time from within QuickBooks, after which it can no longer access any data.
